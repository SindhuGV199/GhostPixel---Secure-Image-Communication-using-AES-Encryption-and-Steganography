The Three Pillars of GhostPixel Security
Defense-in-Depth

1. AES-256-GCM

Confidentiality

Authenticated encryption standard using a 256-bit key derived via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (100,000 rounds) with cryptographically random 16-byte salt and 12-byte nonce. Guarantees unauthorized parties cannot read the secret even if extracted.

2. SHA-256 Hashing

Integrity

Calculates a 256-bit cryptographic digest of the encrypted payload prior to embedding. Extracted data is re-hashed and verified with constant-time equality checks to detect tampering, corruption, or pixel alteration before decryption.

3. RGB LSB Steganography

Concealment

Hides the structured binary packet inside the least significant bits of Red, Green, and Blue pixel channels. Produces a lossless PNG image that appears virtually indistinguishable from the carrier image (PSNR > 65 dB, SSIM > 0.999).
