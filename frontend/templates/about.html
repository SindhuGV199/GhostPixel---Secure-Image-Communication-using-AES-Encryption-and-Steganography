About GhostPixel

An advanced, end-to-end cryptographic covert communications platform combining authenticated AES encryption, payload integrity hashing, and spatial LSB steganography.

Project Mission

In modern adversarial network environments, standard encryption alone is often insufficient. While strong ciphers prevent adversaries from reading plaintext, the mere presence of encrypted traffic draws suspicion and triggers censorship or interception.

GhostPixel solves this dual challenge by pairing cryptography (protecting secrecy) with steganography (protecting privacy by concealing the very existence of the message). An adversary intercepting an innocent image carrier has no statistical or visual indication that a military-grade encrypted message is embedded inside.

Technical Specifications

  • Encryption Cipher: AES-256-GCM (Galois/Counter Mode)
  • Key Derivation: PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (100,000 rounds)
  • Integrity Hash: SHA-256 (256-bit cryptographic digest)
  • Steganography: Spatial RGB 1-LSB sequential matrix modulation
  • Supported Inputs: Lossless PNG, 24-bit TrueColor BMP
  • Output Format: Lossless PNG (to preserve LSB data)
  • Audit Database: Embedded SQLite 3
